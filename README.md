# CORE — Architectural Footwear for Men & Women (Blue & White Edition)

A high-end footwear e-commerce storefront designed for **CORE**, featuring:
- Official **CORE** electric blue brand logo with white star emblem.
- **Electric / Cobalt Blue and Clean White** theme.
- **Sliding horizontal carousels** for all product collections (inspired by Denim Dept).
- **Real MP4 Video Reels** in the lookbook stories section (from Downloads).
- Demo products for both Men and Women (including the 4 uploaded shoes: Fat-Lace Superstar, Air-Stride 90s, Old Skool Canvas, and Shell-Toe Obsidian).

---

## 🎨 Theme & Media Highlights

1. **Official Logo & Color Palette**:
   - Primary: Electric / Cobalt Blue (`#0047ff`) matching the official brand logo.
   - Secondary / Backgrounds: Pure White (`#ffffff`), Soft Ice Blue (`#f0f5ff`, `#e8f0fe`).
   - Deep Midnight Contrast: (`#051336`) for the video reels section and footer.

2. **In-Motion Video Reels (Lookbook Stories Section)**:
   - Added the 4 actual MP4 video files into `assets/videos/`:
     - `reel-1.mp4`: Core Fat-Lace fit check with loose baggy denim.
     - `reel-2.mp4`: Air-Stride retro runner motion on city concrete.
     - `reel-3.mp4`: Old Skool skate canvas and vulcanized waffle sole flex.
     - `reel-4.mp4`: Shell-Toe matte cowhide leather and gold foil inspection.
   - Hover auto-play preview + click to expand into full-screen video reel modal with audio controls and direct "Shop Pair" checkout.

3. **Sliding Experience**:
   - Hero multi-banner slider with auto-play & manual controls.
   - "MUST HAVE" horizontal sliding product track with prev/next buttons.
   - "FEATURED ARCHIVE DROPS" horizontal slider.
   - "KNEE STITCH & HEAVY SOLE" outdoor lawn slider.
   - In-card image angle sliding on hover, swipe, or dot clicks.
